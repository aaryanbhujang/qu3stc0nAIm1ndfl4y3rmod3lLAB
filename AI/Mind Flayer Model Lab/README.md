# Mind Flayer Model Lab 🕳️

*The Upside Down has leaked into our world, and strange neural networks have begun to manifest...*

Deep within the laboratory of Hawkins National Lab, Dr. Brenner's experiments have taken an unexpected turn. The psychic energy from Eleven's abilities has somehow corrupted the AI research division's neural network models. These models now exhibit... peculiar behaviors.

*"Friends don't lie... but neural networks might."*
